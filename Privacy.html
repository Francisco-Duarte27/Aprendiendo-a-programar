Privacy Policy - The Basics

A privacy policy is a statement that discloses some or all of the ways a website collects, uses, discloses, processes, and manages the data of its visitors and customers.

It also includes a statement regarding the website's commitment to protecting privacy and explains the different mechanisms implemented to protect personal information.

What to include in the Privacy Policy

Generally speaking, a Privacy Policy addresses the types of information collected, how it is collected, why it is collected, how it may be shared with third parties and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

Different jurisdictions have different legal obligations, therefore it is important to comply with the legislation applicable to your activities and location.
